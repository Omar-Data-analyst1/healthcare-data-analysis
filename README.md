# healthcare-data-analysis
Project Overview

Healthcare organizations generate large amounts of operational and financial data every day. Without a centralized reporting system, it becomes difficult to monitor hospital performance, patient trends, revenue, and insurance contributions.

In this project, I developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to bring together key healthcare metrics into a single view. The dashboard helps decision-makers analyze revenue performance, patient demographics, hospital efficiency, doctor performance, and admission trends through interactive visualizations.

1.Business Problem
Healthcare data is often spread across multiple sources, making it difficult for management to answer important business questions such as:

📈 Which hospitals generate the highest revenue?
🏥 Which insurance providers contribute the most?
👨‍⚕️ How are patients distributed across age groups and genders?
👩‍⚕️ Which doctors manage the highest patient volumes?
📅 How have revenue trends changed over time?
The objective was to build a dashboard that answers these questions in one place and supports data-driven decision-making.

-Key Business Insights
-Revenue Insights
-Total Revenue: 1.42 Billion
-Insurance providers such as Cigna and Medicare contributed a significant portion of total revenue.
-Revenue showed steady growth from 2019–2023, followed by a decline in 2024, which may indicate incomplete data or operational changes.
-Hospital Insights
-Several hospitals generated more than 1 Million in revenue.
-Hospital-wise comparisons helped identify top-performing facilities.
-Doctor Insights
-Certain doctors handled a higher number of patients while generating greater billing amounts.
-Performance analysis highlighted workload distribution across healthcare providers.
-Patient Insights
-Gender distribution remained nearly balanced.
-Middle-aged and senior patients formed the largest patient groups.
-Blood type and medical condition analysis revealed common treatment patterns.
-Admission Insights
-Elective, Emergency, and Urgent admissions were distributed fairly evenly, indicating a balanced case mix.
